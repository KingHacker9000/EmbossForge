# Calibration workflow

Before printing the full press, calibrate the dimensions that directly affect fit and paper deformation.

## 1. Mechanical fit coupon

The clearance coupon tests nominal radial clearances from 0.10 mm through 0.40 mm. Use the smallest pair that assembles repeatedly without forcing after the parts have fully cooled.

The initial Adventurer 5M / 0.4 mm nozzle working value is 0.25 mm. Treat that only as a starting point.

## 2. Emboss matrix

The emboss matrix varies:

- male relief height: 0.30 to 0.80 mm
- female XY clearance: 0.10 to 0.30 mm
- female extra cavity depth: 0.20 mm

Print the male and female panels, place the actual paper stock between them, and test with light progressive pressure. Choose the combination that produces a clean emboss without tearing, cutting, or sharply creasing the paper.

## 3. Record a printer/material/paper profile

Once a combination works, record:

- printer
- nozzle diameter
- filament material/brand
- layer height
- wall count/infill used for tooling
- measured paper thickness (preferred) or paper GSM
- mechanical slide clearance
- die XY clearance
- male relief height
- female extra cavity depth

Do not assume the same profile transfers exactly to another filament, nozzle, paper stock, or printer.

## Safety

Emboss testing should begin with hand pressure or very low lever force. Printed polymer tooling can crack or eject fragments if overloaded. Keep hands out of the die gap and wear eye protection during early mechanical tests.
