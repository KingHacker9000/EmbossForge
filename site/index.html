NO CAD REQUIRED

From artwork to printable dies in minutes.

The everyday workflow stays deliberately small. Advanced geometry controls exist, but they stay out of the way until you need them.

01

Choose artwork

Drop in SVG, PNG, JPG, WEBP, BMP or TIFF. EmbossForge normalizes the design and prepares it for relief geometry.

02

Pick size & paper

Choose the die diameter and paper type. Printer profiles provide sensible clearance defaults and size checks.

03

Generate the pair

Export a matched male STL and female STL, plus OpenSCAD source and a machine-readable manifest for reproducibility.

BUILT FOR REPEATABILITY

More than a one-off stamp generator.

↔

Matched geometry

Male and female dies are generated as a coordinated pair with paper thickness and configurable clearances.

◇

Artwork normalization

Handles raster tracing, SVG coordinate cleanup and normalized artwork output instead of hiding the preprocessing step.

⌁

Printer profiles

Profiles keep printer-sensitive tolerances explicit and validate geometry against known machine limits.

▣

Desktop + CLI

Use the clean desktop workflow or automate the same backend from the command line and Python API.

⚙

Parametric mechanics

The reusable press, cartridges and receivers are generated from source CAD rather than hand-edited meshes.

INTERCHANGEABLE HARDWARE

One press. Unlimited designs.

EmbossForge is also building a reusable press platform around keyed, swappable die cartridges. Change the design without rebuilding the press.

  • Universal upper and lower cartridge receivers
  • Deterministic keyed orientation
  • Parametric CadQuery press geometry
  • STEP + STL mechanical exports
  • Collision checks for open and closed assemblies

from pathlib import Path
from embossforge.generator import (
    DieGenerationRequest,
    generate_die,
)

result = generate_die(
    DieGenerationRequest(
        artwork=Path("seal.svg"),
        output_root=Path("build"),
        diameter_mm=42,
        paper_preset="copy",
    )
)

print(result.male_stl)
print(result.female_stl)
